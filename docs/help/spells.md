## Spell Slots

Spellcasters have a limited number of spell slots per level. Casting a spell expends one slot of that spell's level or higher. All slots restore on a long rest (and some on short rest for Warlocks).

Spell slot pips are shown on your Character Sheet. The **Cast Spell** action button opens the spell selection dialog.

## Spell Components

- **Verbal (V)** — Requires speech. Cannot cast while Silenced.
- **Somatic (S)** — Requires a free hand.
- **Material (M)** — Requires a listed component or a spellcasting focus.

## Concentration

Some spells require concentration. You can only concentrate on one spell at a time. Concentration breaks if:

- You cast another concentration spell
- You take damage and fail a Constitution saving throw (DC = max(10, half damage taken))
- You are incapacitated or killed

## Cantrips

Cantrips (0-level spells) do not use spell slots and can be cast at will.

## Spell Attack Rolls and Save DCs

- **Spell attack roll** = d20 + spellcasting modifier + proficiency bonus vs. target AC
- **Spell save DC** = 8 + spellcasting modifier + proficiency bonus

## Common Spells by Class

### Wizard
- *Magic Missile* — 3 darts, 1d4+1 force each, auto-hits
- *Fireball* — 8d6 fire in 20 ft. radius, Dex save for half

### Cleric
- *Cure Wounds* — Restore 1d8 + modifier HP on touch
- *Guiding Bolt* — 4d6 radiant; next attack has advantage on target

### Druid
- *Healing Word* — Bonus action; restore 1d4 + modifier HP at range
- *Entangle* — Restrain creatures in 20 ft. square on failed Str save

### Sorcerer / Warlock
- *Eldritch Blast* — 1d10 force cantrip; Warlock staple
- *Chromatic Orb* — 3d8 damage of chosen energy type

See the [Combat](combat.md) page for how spell attack rolls and saving throws work in play.
