# Combat

[← Home](home.md)

## Starting Combat

Combat begins when the DM determines a hostile encounter. The game mode switches to **Combat** and initiative is rolled automatically for all combatants.

## Initiative

Each combatant rolls a d20 and adds their Dexterity modifier. Higher results act first. The initiative order is displayed in the right sidebar and highlighted on the active turn.

## Your Turn

When it is your turn, the action bar activates. You have:

- **1 Action** — Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, or Use an Item
- **1 Bonus Action** — Class features, some spells, off-hand attack
- **1 Reaction** — Opportunity attack or special class features
- **Movement** — Up to your speed in feet

Type your intended action in chat or click an action button.

## Attack Rolls

An attack roll is a d20 + attack modifier vs. the target's Armor Class (AC). A natural 20 is a critical hit — damage dice are doubled.

## Saving Throws

The DM calls for saving throws when your character is affected by spells or hazards. Roll d20 + the relevant ability modifier vs. the DC. The DM resolves the outcome.

## Hit Points and Death

When HP reaches 0, your character falls unconscious. On each of your turns you roll a death saving throw (d20). Three successes = stable. Three failures = dead.

A stable character has 1 HP and is unconscious. Healing magic or a Medicine check restores consciousness.

## Conditions

Conditions are shown as badges on your PlayerCard:

- **Poisoned** — Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks
- **Prone** — Melee attacks against you have advantage; you have disadvantage on attacks
- **Blinded** — Auto-fail sight checks; attacks against you have advantage
- **Restrained** — Speed 0; attacks against you have advantage; your attacks have disadvantage

See [Character](character.md) for a full conditions reference.
